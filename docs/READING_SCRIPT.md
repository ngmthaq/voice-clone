# Voice Recording Script (about 12–15 minutes)

How to read:
- Read at your normal speed, in your normal voice. Do not act.
- Pause 1–2 seconds between paragraphs. Mistakes are fine: pause, then read the sentence again.
- Keep the same distance from the mic (about one hand width).
- Parts marked (softer), (louder), (higher), (lower) help the model learn your full range.

---

## Part 1 — Normal speech

Good morning. My name is not important right now, but my voice is. I am recording this so a computer can learn how I sound.

The weather today is fine. There are a few clouds in the sky, and the wind is soft. Later this afternoon it might rain, so I will take an umbrella.

I usually start the day with a cup of coffee. Sometimes I drink tea instead, especially when it is cold outside. After that, I check my messages and plan the rest of the day.

Yesterday I went to the market. I bought rice, eggs, fresh vegetables, some fruit, and a small bag of coffee beans. The total was about twenty-three dollars and fifty cents.

My phone number is not one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, zero. That is just a list of numbers for practice.

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. A big black bug bit a big black bear. She sells sea shells by the sea shore.

## Part 2 — Questions and answers

Where are you going this weekend? I am not sure yet. Maybe the beach, maybe the mountains.

Did you finish the report? Almost. I need about one more hour.

How much does this cost? It is cheaper than last year, but still not cheap.

Can you hear me clearly? If not, please tell me, and I will move closer to the microphone.

Why is the train late again? Nobody knows. The sign only says "delayed."

What time is the meeting? It starts at nine thirty and should end before eleven.

## Part 3 — Feelings

(happy) That is great news! I am so happy for you. We should celebrate tonight.

(tired) I am really tired today. I did not sleep well, and the day felt very long.

(surprised) Wait, really? I did not expect that at all.

(calm) It is okay. Take a deep breath. We will fix it step by step.

(serious) Please listen carefully. This part is important, and we cannot make mistakes here.

(friendly) Hey, it is good to see you again. How have you been?

## Part 4 — Range

(softer) This sentence is quiet, almost like a whisper, but still clear.

(softer) The baby is sleeping, so please talk quietly in the next room.

(louder) Hello! Can everyone in the back hear me?

(louder) Watch out! The ball is coming this way!

(higher) Oh, that is so cute! Look at that little cat!

(lower) Now my voice is lower and slower, like a narrator in a long film.

Normal again. I will keep reading in my everyday voice for the rest of this script.

## Part 5 — Short story

Every morning, an old man walked to the same small bridge near his house. He stood in the middle, looked at the water, and counted the boats that passed under him.

Most days there were three or four boats. On busy days there were more than ten. He wrote each number in a small notebook that he kept in his jacket pocket.

One morning, a young girl stopped next to him. "What are you counting?" she asked.

"Boats," he said. "I have counted them for twenty years."

"Why?" she asked.

He smiled and closed his notebook. "Because one day, I want to know if the river is getting busier, or if I am just getting older."

The girl laughed. The next morning she came back with her own notebook. From that day, they counted the boats together.

## Part 6 — Work and technology

Please open the settings menu and select the audio tab. From there, choose your microphone and set the volume to about seventy percent.

The computer is updating. Do not turn it off. This may take several minutes.

Our team meets every Monday to review last week's work and plan the next steps. We use short notes and simple lists, so everyone can follow.

If the application stops responding, close it, wait a few seconds, and open it again. If the problem continues, check the log file.

Artificial intelligence can be useful, but it should be used carefully and honestly. A cloned voice should never be used to trick or hurt other people.

## Part 7 — Mixed sentences

I would like a table for two, please.

The library closes at eight o'clock on weekdays and at five o'clock on weekends.

Turn left at the second traffic light, then go straight for about three hundred meters.

Remember to bring your passport, your ticket, and a charger for your phone.

The museum has paintings, old maps, and a large collection of musical instruments.

Thank you very much for your help. I really appreciate it.

Sorry, I did not catch that. Could you say it one more time?

Let me think about it, and I will call you back tomorrow afternoon.

## Part 8 — Free talk (2–3 minutes)

Now talk freely, with no script. Some ideas:
- Describe your home, your street, or your city.
- Talk about your favourite food and how to cook it.
- Explain your job or a hobby to a friend.
- Tell a short story about a trip you remember.

When you finish, say: "This is the end of the recording." Then stop the recording.
